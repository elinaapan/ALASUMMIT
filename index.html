Trail planning & preparation

Choose only a route appropriate to your experience. Trail access and conditions can change.

Day hikes

Check official access, daylight, weather and your group’s fitness. Carry an offline track from a trusted source.

Long ridge routes

Plan a conservative turnaround time, keep the group together and tell a trusted adult your plan and return time.

High-altitude / technical peaks

Amangeldi and Talgar are serious objectives, not casual hikes. Use qualified mountain professionals and a verified current route plan; do not go alone.

What works offline?
Peak descriptions, safety notes, favorites and a GPX track you import can be available locally. Photos still load from the web in this version; a full offline basemap and guaranteed offline GPS require locally bundled assets and compatible device/browser support.
